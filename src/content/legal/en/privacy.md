We respect your privacy. This page explains what data Dreddi collects, why, who we share it with and how to delete it.

## What we collect
- **Account:** email, profile name and handle, sign-in method (email, Google or Apple). For Apple sign-in we keep a token so we can revoke access when you delete your account.
- **Profile:** fields of activity and social links, if you added them
- **Promises:** text, deadlines, participants, statuses and confirmations
- **Disputes:** description, the sides' positions, arguments and Dreddi's verdict
- **Telegram:** when @dreddi_bot is called in a group, we process only the messages addressed to it: the call and replies to the bot. We store the Telegram name of those who picked a position. Telegram IDs are stored only as a hash.
- **Technical data:** notification settings and usage data for stable operation

## How we use data
- To run the service: promises, disputes, reputation and notifications
- **For verdicts:** the dispute text and arguments are sent to Anthropic's Claude model. Under Anthropic's terms, data sent through the API is not used to train models.
- For emails about notifications and your account
- For security and abuse prevention, including daily limits on calling Dreddi

## What others see
- **Public profile:** name, handle, fields of activity, promise stats and reputation. It is on by default and can be turned off in settings.
- A public promise page is visible to anyone with the link
- **A dispute verdict** is visible to anyone with the link. Telegram names are hidden on the site.

## Who we share data with
We don't sell data. We share it only with the services Dreddi can't run without:

- Supabase: database and sign-in
- Vercel: hosting
- Anthropic: AI
- Resend: email
- Telegram: the bot
- Google and Apple: sign-in

## Retention and deletion
- **Deleting your account:** you can delete it in settings. For 30 days you can restore it just by signing in again. After that the data is deleted for good.
- **Shared promises:** promises with other people stay with them, and «Deleted account» is shown in your place.

## Your rights
- Change your profile data and turn off your public profile and notifications in settings
- Get a copy of your data or ask a question at support@dreddi.com
